      Method and limitations
      
This verifier checks a mytracker proof export, including archive entry hashes, canonical location digests and the attached TSA and OTS proofs. Historical verification labels are not accepted as fresh evidence.

      
TSA checks use the selected trust roots, allowed policies and archived nonce. This verifier does not use the browser or OS certificate store and does not check certificate revocation.

      
Offline OTS checks cannot confirm a Bitcoin anchor. Online checks require at least two agreeing providers and the selected confirmation threshold. It does not validate parent links, cumulative chainwork or the full Bitcoin chain. Providers must allow browser CORS requests.

      
A timestamp supports that a digest existed by an attested time. It does not authenticate the archive author or establish that the reported location, identity or event time is true. Obtain this verifier and trust roots from sources you trust.

      
Canonical hashing covers user, device, time, coordinates and supported measurements, not every payload field. See the proof format, RFC 3161 and OpenTimestamps.
